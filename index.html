Instantaneous conversion

Projected incident solar power = irradiance × area × cosine of roof incidence angle, in W.

PPV,DC = G(t) × A × incidence × (1 − shade) × ηPV,25°C × derating.

Derating = max(0, 1 − 0.004 × (cell temperature − 25°C)). Inverter AC = DC input × inverter efficiency, limited to the rated AC output. Panels face south at a fixed tilt of 25°.

Energy & storage

E (Wh) = ΣP (W) × Δt (h), with Δt = 0.25 h. The battery is modeled as 5 kWh, 1 kW DC charge/discharge cap and 95% charge/discharge efficiency.

PV DC = PV DC to inverter + battery charging DC + DC curtailed. Inverter DC = PV DC to inverter + battery discharging DC. Inverter AC = home supplied AC + grid export AC. Home load = home supplied AC + grid import AC.

Scope and limitations: Idealized 06:00–18:00 clear-sky day; irradiance follows a smooth synthetic curve; no weather history, temperature variation over the day, detailed solar geometry, shading geometry, battery aging, switching dynamics, auxiliary loads, DC cable losses, tariffs or real-site calibration. The uniform shading percentage is a simplified multiplier—not a prediction for partial shading in real strings. The 3D representation is schematic, not a spatially calibrated building model.
